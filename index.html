Introduction and Learning Philosophy

My journey into computer engineering began with a fascination for not just *using* technology, but understanding *how* it works at a fundamental level. This curiosity led me to build an 8-bit computer from scratch, giving me a deep appreciation for the layers of abstraction that make modern software possible.

My philosophy is that true mastery comes from bridging the gap between hardware and software. I believe in hands-on learning, whether that means debugging a circuit board or developing a full-stack web application. I am driven to create solutions that are not only functional and efficient but also intuitive and accessible.
