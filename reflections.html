Collaborative and Leadership Experiences

During my industrial training at iTechBrains, I worked solo on Transcodex, a full-stack media management platform, while regularly coordinating with a faculty supervisor and an industry project manager to align on requirements and progress. Designing the storage layer as a pluggable, factory-pattern adapter (rather than hard-coding local storage) taught me to plan for change even on a single-developer project, and to communicate technical decisions clearly to non-implementing stakeholders.

Global Awareness and Ethical Considerations

While building web applications, I've become increasingly aware of the ethical implications of data privacy. It's not just a technical requirement to secure user data; it's an ethical one. This has influenced my work by making me prioritize security and transparency. For example, in a personal project, I chose to minimize the data I collected and was transparent in the UI about why it was needed. As engineers, we have a responsibility to consider the global impact and potential misuse of our technology.

Future Aspirations and Professional Development

My immediate goal is to build on my industrial training at iTechBrains with a software engineering internship where I can contribute to larger systems and learn from senior engineers. I am particularly interested in backend development, distributed systems, and applied NLP/ML.

Long-term, I aim to specialize in system architecture or cloud computing. I've already completed NPTEL coursework in Advanced Distributed Systems and Cloud Computing, and plan to continue building complex projects and deepening my systems knowledge in my spare time.

Conclusion and Self-Assessment

Summary: This portfolio documents my journey from understanding low-level hardware to building modern, full-stack applications. Each project has been a stepping stone, reinforcing my belief in hands-on learning and my passion for solving complex problems.
