Collaborative and Leadership Experiences

During my web development internship, I was part of a four-person "feature" team. My primary role was frontend development, but I was also responsible for communicating API requirements to the backend developer. We faced a challenge when our data models didn't match, which I helped resolve by organizing a joint session to map out the data flow. This experience taught me that clear communication and a shared understanding are just as important as clean code in a team's success.

Global Awareness and Ethical Considerations

While building web applications, I've become increasingly aware of the ethical implications of data privacy. It's not just a technical requirement to secure user data; it's an ethical one. This has influenced my work by making me prioritize security and transparency. For example, in a personal project, I chose to minimize the data I collected and was transparent in the UI about why it was needed. As engineers, we have a responsibility to consider the global impact and potential misuse of our technology.

Future Aspirations and Professional Development

My immediate goal is to secure a software engineering internship where I can contribute to large-scale systems and learn from senior engineers. I am particularly interested in backend development and distributed systems.

Long-term, I aim to specialize in system architecture or cloud computing. To get there, I plan to deepen my knowledge of tools like Kubernetes and Kafka, pursue advanced cloud certifications, and continue building complex, high-availability projects in my spare time.
